01 / THE EXPERIENCE

Just the essentials.
Nothing in the way.

The little details turn a familiar clock into something that feels right at home on your desktop.

✳

A screen worth returning to.

Split-flap movement with a carefully timed 330 ms transition. Switch off animation whenever you prefer stillness.

◷

On your schedule.

Appears when your GNOME session is idle and steps aside when you're back. Lock-screen and power controls stay with GNOME.

Personal by design.

Feels like GNOME.

Native GTK settings, extension-native preferences, multiple displays and light/dark appearance.

◎

Yours, entirely.
